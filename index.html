作家About

I am Hideaki Izumi (泉 英明), an artist and AI director living in Odawara — the old castle town of the Hojo clan, at the gate of Hakone and Mount Fuji. As a personal project, I share the beautiful and fun sides of Japan with people around the world.

The shop opened on October 1, 2026. Every piece carries my seal 泉 (Izumi), and every place is named with its real prefecture.

How the art is made: I do all the planning, concept and design of every work myself, and the pictures are created with AI image tools. They are not hand-painted. 作品の企画・立案・デザインはすべて私自身で行い、絵の制作にはAIを使っています。 Scenes with Christmas lights or historical figures are imagined, not photographs of present-day Japan, and each listing says so.

小田原（神奈川県）に住む泉 英明（いずみ ひであき）です。芸者・美人画、箱根・小田原・富士山の墨絵、東海道の浮世絵風、大正ロマンの箱根、日本のクリスマス、2027年カレンダーを、Samsung Frame TV や印刷向けのデジタルデータで販売しています。国内のお客様は BASE から日本語でご購入いただけます。
